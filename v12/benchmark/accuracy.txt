V12 numerical validation

Retained precision contract
- Dataset training copy: FP32.
- Forward activations: FP32.
- Adaptive sigmoid: FP32 polynomial or FP32 libmvec expf fallback.
- Backpropagation: FP32.
- Per-thread gradients: FP32.
- Cross-thread gradient promotion/reduction: FP64.
- Master weights: FP64.
- Momentum state: FP64.
- Weight update: inherited FP64 v11 update.
- Final inference and target confirmation: inherited exact FP64 path.
