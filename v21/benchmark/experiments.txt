V21 target-aware Gauss-Newton refresh investigation
===================================================

Baseline
--------
V20 computes one median-normalised square-root inverse Gauss-Newton diagonal
when PE first reaches 0.05%. The scale is clipped to [1/28, 28] and held fixed.
V19 history staging, v18 curvature acceptance, Armijo and MSE remain unchanged.

Hypothesis
----------
The v20 diagonal is local curvature information. If the deep tail remains
ill-conditioned while parameters move materially away from the 0.05% state,
recomputing the same diagonal a small number of times may restore useful
coordinate scaling without changing the secant history.

Initial refresh screening
-------------------------
A single extra refresh was tested at PE values from roughly 0.04% down to
0.002%. The best threshold varied with row count. Refreshes around 0.02-0.03%
were promising, while a first refresh at 0.005% was generally too late.

Two-refresh screening
---------------------
The combination 0.0225% then 0.01% was retained after cross-scale screening.
The second threshold was sensitive: on 5k rows, alternatives at 0.011%, 0.009%
and 0.0075% required materially more evaluations to reach 0.001%.

Target gate
-----------
The extra refreshes are enabled only when the requested target is <=0.001%.
This was added because applying the refresh schedule to a requested 0.002%
target could regress the 5k case. For targets >0.001%, production v21 returns
directly to trainRangeV20.

Ultra-deep third refresh
------------------------
For requested targets <=0.0005%, an additional refresh at PE <=0.002% was
consistently beneficial across 5k, 20k and 100k. Candidate refreshes at 0.005%
and 0.003% were weaker in the cross-scale screen.

Strict AVX2/libmvec confirmation
--------------------------------
The retained comparison used benchmark/refresh_benchmark.cpp compiled with:
  g++ -std=c++11 -O3 -mavx2 -mfma -fopenmp \
      -Wall -Wextra -Wpedantic -Werror refresh_benchmark.cpp -lmvec

No -ffast-math flag was used for the retained benchmark table.

0.001% target:
  rows=5000   v20=2781  v21=1211  reduction=56.5%
  rows=20000  v20=1829  v21=1166  reduction=36.2%
  rows=100000 v20=1227  v21=998   reduction=18.7%

0.0005% target:
  rows=5000   v20=6228  v21=2248  reduction=63.9%
  rows=20000  v20=7431  v21=3812  reduction=48.7%
  rows=100000 v20=3526  v21=2593  reduction=26.5%

Each objective/gradient evaluation counts as one row-pass equivalent and each
Gauss-Newton diagonal refresh counts as one additional row-pass equivalent.

Production policy
-----------------
Requested target > 0.001%:
  delegate directly to v20.

Requested target <= 0.001%:
  v20 initial GN scale at 0.05%;
  refresh at 0.0225%;
  refresh at 0.01%.

Requested target <= 0.0005%:
  additionally refresh at 0.002%.

The refresh recomputes the existing v20 scale. It does not reset history,
change the line search, modify curvature acceptance, or change stopping and
final-confirmation semantics.
