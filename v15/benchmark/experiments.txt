V15 experiment log

Retained: parallel exact FP64 L-BFGS evaluation
- Same v14 L-BFGS optimiser and target.
- Objective/gradient work partitioned across deterministic contiguous row ranges.
- FP64 gradients per worker, long-double metric accumulators.
- Fixed-order reduction after each evaluation.
- Cap at four evaluator workers.

Thread-count screen, 500,000 rows
- 1 thread: median ~0.372 s
- 2 threads: median ~0.197 s
- 4 threads: median ~0.182 s
- 5 threads: median ~0.187 s
Decision: retain four workers on this five-core host.

Rejected: FP32 proposal gradient + FP64 Armijo acceptance
- Proposal directions were computed from FP32 gradients.
- Every candidate still required an exact FP64 objective/gradient evaluation for Armijo acceptance and stopping.
- All screened cases still reached target in three exact evaluations.
- The extra proposal evaluations therefore duplicated work.
- At 1m rows, 4 threads:
  exact-only median ~0.357 s
  hybrid proposal median ~0.711 s
Decision: reject.

Release benchmark
- Seven alternating paired runs per size.
- Baseline: same exact L-BFGS evaluator, one worker.
- Candidate: same evaluator and optimiser, four workers.
- Both reached target on evaluation 3 in every retained run.
