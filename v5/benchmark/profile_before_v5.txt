V4 profile that motivated V5
============================

Workload: 1,000,000 rows, 40 updates, four threads, accelerated v4 path.
Approximate CPU-time breakdown:

W1/W2 backpropagation and gradient accumulation   41.7%
Hidden-layer forward multiply                     22.8%
Hidden sigmoid / vector exp                       13.0%
OpenMP runtime and synchronisation                12.2%
Error / percentage diagnostics                     4.6%
Output dot product and output sigmoid               3.5%

The main conclusion was that roughly two thirds of v4 CPU time had moved into ordinary multiply-add work after v4 vectorised sigmoid evaluation. V5 therefore targets the fixed 11 x 16 arithmetic directly rather than changing the sigmoid again.
