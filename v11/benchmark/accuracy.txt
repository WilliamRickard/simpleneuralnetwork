- Middle path: degree-8 polynomial on 1 < |x| <= 4.
- Outer path: degree-8 polynomial on 4 < |x| <= 12.
- Saturation outside |x| > 12.
- Dense-grid validation on [-20, 20]: maximum absolute sigmoid error = 6.566964233945677e-06.

Stopping semantics
- Training uses the approximate sigmoid in v11 gradient passes.
- If the approximate percentage-error metric indicates that the target may have been reached, v11 recomputes metrics with the exact existing inference path before accepting the stopping condition.
