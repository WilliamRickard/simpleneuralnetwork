V20 deep-convergence investigation
==================================

Baseline
--------
V19 staged L-BFGS: history 10 above 0.05% PE, history 160 thereafter.
V18 scale-aware curvature test, Armijo search and MSE objective retained.

Rejected history-management experiments
---------------------------------------
1. Reject highly collinear s-pairs.
   Helped early, then starved the history and stalled the tail.
2. Replace the most similar stored s-pair.
   Strong 5k results, but not robust at 20k 0.001%.
3. Curvature-angle quality eviction.
   Lost chronological secant information and converged poorly.
4. Periodic redundancy replacement.
   Did not improve the 20k tail.
5. Direction-quality-triggered replacement.
   Did not improve the 20k tail.
6. Two-timescale history with downsampled old pairs.
   Rejected in screening.
7. Late history expansion 160 -> 192.
   Worse than v19 history 160.
8. Dense BFGS continuation below 0.005%.
   Small roughly 1-2% gain only, below the release bar.

Conditioning diagnosis
----------------------
At low error, a Gauss-Newton approximation was extremely ill-conditioned. The
measured spectrum spanned roughly 13 orders of magnitude, while the diagonal
alone varied by roughly 1.9e4. This motivated changing the initial L-BFGS
inverse-Hessian scale rather than deleting curvature pairs.

Retained preconditioner
-----------------------
At the first current state with PE <= 0.05%, compute one exact row pass of the
prediction Jacobian diagonal:
    d_i = mean_r (d yhat_r / d theta_i)^2
Use the median m of d and set
    scale_i = clip(sqrt(m / max(d_i, 1e-18*m)), 1/28, 28)
Then use gamma * scale_i in the initial matrix of the normal two-loop L-BFGS
recursion. The scale is fixed for the remainder of the run.

Why square-root scaling
-----------------------
Full inverse-diagonal scaling was less robust. The square-root transform
reduces anisotropy while avoiding an aggressive approximate Newton step.

Deterministic AVX2/libmvec screening
------------------------------------
All comparisons below use the same four-worker FP64 screening evaluator and
count the one Gauss-Newton pass as one row-pass equivalent.

Clip sensitivity
----------------
The best clip is not monotonic across row counts. At 20k, clip 32 was faster
than 16; at 100k, clip 28 was materially faster than 32. Clip 28 was retained
as the cross-scale robustness choice. Very large clips saturated and then
worsened the trajectory.

Fast-path contract
------------------
No preconditioner is computed until the current state is already at or below
0.05% PE and the requested target requires another iteration. The code calls
directionV14 directly when no diagonal is active, preserving the existing
v19 direction calculation above the threshold.
