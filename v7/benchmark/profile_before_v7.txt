V6 stage profile used to choose the v7 work
============================================

Environment: Intel Xeon Platinum 8573C, 5 exposed physical cores
Workload: 1,000,000 rows, 20 updates
Method: sampled stage timing inside the AVX-512 training block

Approximate median share of sampled block time:

Stage                                  1 thread    4 threads
Hidden sigmoid / vector exp              29.1%        38.4%
11 x 16 hidden forward multiply          30.3%        18.5%
Fused backprop + W1/W2 gradients         15.0%        17.9%
Percentage-error + deltaThree            10.4%         9.0%
Output sigmoid                            7.2%         6.9%
Output dot product                        6.7%         6.4%

Interpretation:
- v6 successfully reduced the backprop/W1-gradient bottleneck.
- hidden sigmoid remained the largest multicore hotspot.
- the forward multiply became the largest single-thread arithmetic hotspot.
- the metric/deltaThree loop remained large enough to justify vectorisation.
