V9 profiling basis and generated-code inspection
Environment: Intel Xeon Platinum 8573C, GCC 14.2.0, Linux x86-64, glibc libmvec

Measured hotspot basis
- The committed v8 precursor profile showed hidden + output sigmoid at about 45.2% of sampled single-thread cycles and 49.4% of sampled four-thread cycles.
- Forward 11x16 multiplication accounted for about 20.4% of sampled single-thread cycles.
- Backpropagation and gradient accumulation accounted for about 18.7% of sampled single-thread cycles.

Source-level findings
- The v8 AVX-512 tile carries 22 W1 gradient vectors and 2 W2 gradient vectors.
- Loading and storing those 24 vectors once per 16-row tile moves 3,072 bytes of accumulator state per tile.
- At 1,000,000 rows there are 62,500 complete tiles, equivalent to 192,000,000 bytes, or about 183.1 MiB, of accumulator load/store traffic per update.

Generated-code finding
- Assembly inspection of the benchmark binary found 104 ZMM instructions referencing stack locations.
- This indicates the existing kernel already operates under meaningful AVX-512 register pressure.
- A proposed cross-tile carry of the 24-vector gradient state was therefore rejected before implementation because extending vector lifetimes was likely to increase spilling.

V9 optimisation target
- Keep the arithmetic and libmvec exponential unchanged.
- Reduce overhead around the dominant sigmoid stage using a fixed full-tile path.
- Omit the per-vector -700 exceptional check only when a conservative pass-level bound proves it unreachable.
- Preserve v8 fallbacks when the proof or workload dispatch condition is not met.
