V9 exactness validation

Method
- Independent equivalence harness initialises v8 and v9 from identical deterministic state.
- It executes the active v9 workload path and the corresponding v8 path.
- It compares W1, W2, momentum/delta W1 and momentum/delta W2 byte-for-byte.

Results
