V13 experiments

Retained
1. BF16 W1/input operands with VDPBF16PS FP32 accumulation.
2. FP32 sigmoid, W2 forward, hidden delta and gradients unchanged from v12.
3. FP64 master weights, momentum and update unchanged.
4. 16-row forward grouping for retained single-thread full tiles.
5. Runtime AVX512_BF16 capability gate.
6. v12 fallback for all non-single-thread and unsupported paths.

Rejected / not retained
1. BF16 W1 forward with one-row weight reloads.
   - Positive but left avoidable load overhead.
2. Four-row and eight-row grouping as the universal single-thread choice.
   - Competitive, but 16-row grouping gave the best overall retained single-thread result.
3. BF16 paired W1-gradient accumulation.
   - Sometimes another 1-3 percentage points single-threaded.
   - Inconsistent at larger/multi-thread workloads and broadens the precision compromise.
4. Four-thread BF16 dispatch.
   - No stable release-quality paired result.
5. Reconstruct gradient inputs from BF16 instead of retaining v12 FP32 inputs.
   - Reduced memory but erased most of the performance gain.
6. Global BF16 compiler target.
   - Rejected in favour of function target attributes plus runtime detection, preserving portable fallback.
