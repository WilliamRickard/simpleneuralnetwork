V26 profiling and dense-algebra experiments
===========================================

Retained candidate
------------------
Use a column-major 192x192 dense inverse-Hessian after the existing v25 dense
handover. AVX-512 lanes represent independent matrix rows, while each lane
accumulates j=0..191 in the same order as the scalar v22 implementation.

Required exactness controls:
- no horizontal SIMD reduction for H*x;
- separate multiply then add, not FMA;
- function-local optimize("fp-contract=off") on AVX-512 helpers;
- keep dotV14, curvature checks, y^T H y and BFGS coefficients on the inherited
  generic code path.

The diagonal v23 seed is representation-independent, so no transpose is needed
at initialisation or reset.

Exactness results
-----------------
Focused benchmark:
- initial dense direction: 0 differing doubles;
- one inverse-BFGS update: 0 differing matrix elements;
- after 100 repeated direction/update cycles: still 0 differences.

Production-style full optimiser harness, both deep targets at 5k/10k/20k/
50k/100k:
- updates identical;
- evaluator/reject counts identical;
- final percentage error identical;
- final objective identical.

Five-run focused timing median
------------------------------
scalar direction: 18.257037750 us
v26 direction:     7.586156083 us
speedup:           2.3192x

scalar update:     35.618068083 us
v26 update:        20.195069667 us
speedup:           1.7758x

combined dense algebra: 1.9513x faster.

Production impact model
-----------------------
The standalone end-to-end research harness uses an AVX2 evaluator and can incur
AVX2/AVX-512 transition effects. Its wall times are therefore not used as
production speed claims.

The retained component model combines measured production-style AVX-512
objective/gradient pass times with the dense timings above. Since trajectories
are exact, pass counts are unchanged. Estimated runtime reductions range from
about 13-15% at 5k, 8-9% at 10k, 4-5% at 20k, 2% at 50k and about 1% at 100k.
See exact_dense_summary.csv.

Earlier profiling and rejected candidates
-----------------------------------------
1. Persistent OpenMP evaluator team:
   entering a warm four-thread region cost only about 1.5-2.6 us. This is about
   1-2% of a 5k evaluator pass and negligible at 100k, so it is not the main
   v26 opportunity.
2. Remove PE/max-error bookkeeping:
   effects were noisy and mostly single-digit; reduce-max was not better.
3. Faster sigmoid/exp:
   a cheap timing-only sigmoid suggested 35-40% evaluator upside, but no
   accurate faster vector-exp backend was available in the installed toolchain.
   Prior exp2 substitution changed optimiser trajectories and remains rejected.
4. Row-major AVX-512 dense algebra:
   horizontal reductions/FMA gave around 2.2x dense speed but changed the
   numerical trajectory. Rejected.
5. Column-major AVX-512 without fp-contract=off:
   direction initially matched, but the BFGS update differed by around one ulp
   in many cells and divergence accumulated. Rejected.
