V28 evaluator experiments
=========================

Baseline
--------
v27 merge commit:
73ee69510c9b2651cef6084556d70f1a73a39923

v27 production blob:
f45d98227df884671bb762e07c0e07b27a6b5ae1

Initial profile
---------------
At 100k rows on the four-worker benchmark configuration, a cheap-sigmoid timing surrogate reduced a full evaluator pass from about 1.17 ms to about 0.55 ms. A first-layer isolation test put hidden sigmoid work at roughly 0.43 ms and backpropagation at roughly 0.32 ms.

Activation ranges
-----------------
Synthetic deep-training hidden preactivations stayed within roughly |z| < 1.82.

The repeated real-Wine workload was much wider by the 0.0005% stage:
median |z| ~ 5.37
p90 |z| ~ 14.72
p99 |z| ~ 30.99
max |z| ~ 50.91

Only about 66% of hidden preactivations were inside [-8,8], while about 92% were inside [-16,16].

Rejected: approximate vector sigmoid
------------------------------------
A range-reduced AVX-512 2^x-polynomial sigmoid produced:
- evaluator speedup roughly 1.30x to 1.36x;
- worst observed relative error below about 9e-13 across five million samples on [-60,60].

Despite that numerical accuracy, Wine 13.3k / 0.001% changed basin:
- baseline hold-out classification accuracy: about 84.4%;
- approximate-sigmoid candidate: about 71.1%.

Rejected.

Rejected: noplt
---------------
Changing the libmvec external call form to avoid PLT indirection was bit-exact and effectively neutral. The cost is inside vector exp, not dynamic symbol dispatch.

Rejected: restricted-domain libmvec clone
-----------------------------------------
glibc's AVX-512 exp already uses table-based range reduction, a short polynomial and bit construction of the exponent, with exceptional scalar fallbacks outside the normal domain. Reimplementing that logic locally would add maintenance/licensing burden for limited expected gain.

Register-pressure investigation
-------------------------------
The v17 evaluator holds 22 first-layer gradient ZMM accumulators across repeated external vector-exp calls. SysV vector registers are caller-saved, causing substantial spill/reload traffic.

Rejected intermediate candidates:
- all-scratch first-layer gradients: too much L1 traffic;
- KEEP=5 hybrid: exact and faster, but less stable than the final design;
- row-major immediate update of spilled pairs: exact but close to neutral;
- vector reduction of maxPercentageError: exact but no useful combined gain.

Retained: explicit call-boundary evaluator
------------------------------------------
For each eight-row tile:
1. compute forward preactivations;
2. store preactivation state before libmvec calls;
3. compute/store hidden activations;
4. compute output sigmoid;
5. only then load the gradient accumulators;
6. process all eight rows of backprop in original order;
7. store gradients once.

This preserves every accumulator's FMA sequence.

Assembly evidence
-----------------
Production-style slice, exploratory GCC assembly:
- total stack references: 327 -> 265;
- ZMM stack references: 274 -> 191;
- ZMM stack traffic reduction: about 30%.

Exactness
---------
Fresh production-kernel benchmark:
- 425/425 cases passed;
- row counts 1, 7, 8, 9, 127, 133, 999, 1000, 2048, 3072,
  5000, 10000, 10003, 13300, 20005, 50000, 100000;
- worker partitions 1 through 5;
- five deterministic seeds;
- zero differences in metrics or any gradient double.

Earlier wider stress:
- 756/756 cases passed.

Full optimiser:
- 5k/10k/20k/50k/100k deep-target trajectories remained bit-identical;
- accepted updates, evaluator calls, rejects, PE, objective and final 192 parameters identical.

Wine:
- both 0.001% and 0.0005% targets bit-identical;
- final weights identical;
- hold-out RMSE identical;
- classification accuracy identical at 84.44%.

Performance
-----------
Fresh 100k single-worker production-kernel benchmark on the later, more-contended host:
median speedup ~1.059x.

Earlier cooled single-worker evaluator medians:
- 5k ~1.094x
- 10k ~1.162x
- 20k ~1.113x
- 50k ~1.090x
- 100k ~1.059x

Three-worker quota-headroom tests were positive at every tested size, generally around 1.065x-1.094x.

Long 0.0005% synthetic full-training medians were positive at all production sizes, but several larger values were visibly inflated by host scheduling noise and are not used as expected performance claims.

Wine, seven cooled rotating-order pairs:
- 0.001% median ~1.105x
- 0.0005% median ~1.078x

Release interpretation
----------------------
Expected gain: high-single-digit whole-training improvement on representative deep workloads, with no numerical change.

Do not advertise the largest individual synthetic timing wins. The benchmark host's four-CPU quota creates substantial sustained-load variance.
