V18 error-floor experiments

Baseline
- v17 target 0.02%, 100k rows, 500 accepted-iteration cap: final ~0.035459%; target not reached.
- Raising the iteration cap alone does not remove the plateau.

Capacity check
- Benchmark outputs are generated by an 11 -> 16 -> 1 sigmoid network.
- Evaluating the generating weights gives exactly 0 objective and 0% percentage error.
- Therefore ~0.0354% is not a representational floor.

Rejected optimiser variants
- L-BFGS history 5: ~0.035481% after 500 iterations.
- history 20: ~0.035462%.
- history 40: ~0.035462%.
- periodic history reset every 10/20/50/100 iterations: ~0.03547-0.03579%.
- Armijo c1 1e-6 versus 1e-4: no material change.
- naive strong-Wolfe test: substantially worse.
- safeguarded zoom strong-Wolfe crossed 0.02%, but required more evaluations than retained scale-aware curvature.

Root-cause experiment
- v17 curvature gate: s.y > 1e-12.
- retained v18 gate: s.y > 1e-8 * ||s|| * ||y||, with s.y > 0.
- Relative eps values 1e-8 through 1e-14 gave the same convergence trajectory in screening.
- 1e-8 retained as a conservative dimensionless positive-curvature safeguard.

Deep convergence
- 100k, target 0.02%: ~0.019979%, 492 evaluations.
- 100k, target 0.01%: ~0.009983%, 2341 evaluations.
- 1m, target 0.02%: ~0.019991%, 442 evaluations.
- A 2000-accepted-iteration screen did not reach 0.005%; further optimiser work is needed below ~0.01%.

Fast-path non-regression
- 3.9% remains 3 evaluations.
- 1% remains 12 evaluations.
- 0.1% remains 18 evaluations.
- 0.05% remains 19 evaluations.
