V19 deep-convergence experiments

Starting point
- v18 uses scale-aware curvature acceptance and history 10.
- v18 preserves the fast path but deep convergence becomes expensive around 0.01%.

Post-v18 history screen
- 20k rows, history 10, 0.005% target, 3000 accepted-update cap: target missed; ~0.00623% final PE.
- Larger histories materially improved the deep tail only after the v18 curvature fix.
- History 80 crossed 0.005% in screening far earlier than history 10.
- History 160 was the best retained deep cap after sensitivity checks against 120 and 192.
- History 192 did not improve the 100k 0.001% screen: ~3516 evaluations versus ~3197 for history 160.

Stage threshold screen
- Keep history 10 above 0.05% and expand only for deeper targets.
- Entering at 0.05% materially outperformed entering at 0.02% or 0.01% on the 20k 0.001% screen.
- The deep stage is sticky once entered so a temporary PE fluctuation cannot throw away accumulated deep curvature information.

Independent FP64 re-run
- Deterministic 20k dataset using the documented generating weights and initial weights.
- Objective: 0.5 * mean squared error.
- Gradient: exact FP64 algebra for the 11 -> 16 -> 1 sigmoid network.
- Line search: Armijo c1=1e-4, initial step 1, halving, at most 20 trial evaluations.
- Curvature: s.y > 0 and s.y > 1e-8 * ||s|| * ||y||.

Earlier C++ screening
- 100k staged 10 -> 160 reached ~0.00099488% in ~3197 evaluations.
- 100k history 10 missed 0.005% after 2000 accepted updates / ~2881 evaluations, ending around 0.00805%.
- 1m staged 10 -> 160 crossed 0.02% at ~100 evaluations.
- 1m was ~0.00563% after 400 accepted updates and ~0.00529% after 500.
- Long-tail 1m wall timings were too variable in the available execution environment for a defensible timing comparison, so no 1m timing speedup is claimed.

Diagnosis
- Curvature rejection is not the active limiter in the retained 20k run.
- Non-descent resets are not the active limiter.
- Armijo generally accepts near-full steps in the useful deep trajectory.
- W1 and W2 gradient norms remain of comparable order.
- Stored deep steps become redundant: the 160-step history had effective numerical rank around 97 in one 0.001% conditioning check, with pairwise step cosines approaching ~0.98.
- The next likely improvement is history quality/compression rather than a more expensive generic line search.
